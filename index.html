About Xtion Jerry

Xtion Jerry is a South Sudanese music producer, sound engineer, musician, and video editor driven by a passion for sound, creativity, and visual storytelling.

From producing beats and engineering records to creating music and visual content, Xtion Jerry brings together music, technology, and creativity to turn ideas into powerful experiences.

His work is built around one goal: creating authentic sounds and visuals that connect with people.

Based in South Sudan and connected to the growing African creative scene, Xtion Jerry continues to produce, collaborate, and push his creativity beyond boundaries.
