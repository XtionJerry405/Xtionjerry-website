ABOUT THE ARTIST

Xtion Jerry is an artist, songwriter, producer and sound engineer bringing his own sound, energy and creativity into African music.

His music combines modern African sounds with dancehall, Afrobeat, reggae and other influences.

His goal is simple: create music that people can feel, remember and connect with.
